## Higher Education Training
My pedagogical training through the [**Higher Education Teaching Certificates in Hessen**](https://www.hda.tu-darmstadt.de/hochschuldidaktik/angebote_hd/zertifikat_hd/index.en.jsp) covers core components of academic instruction, curriculum design, student assessment, and interactive lecturing formats.

- **Professional Competences (Hessen Certificate)**  
Advanced focus on seminar design, activating large and small student groups, constructive alignment in syllabus building, and modern digital tools for science education.
- **Basic Competences**    
Foundational methodologies in university-level communication, presentation architecture, and inclusive classroom management.
