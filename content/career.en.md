My doctoral research at TU Darmstadt focuses on the electronic structure, spectroscopic parameters, and reactivity of transition-metal complexes using modern wavefunction and multireference quantum chemical methods.  

In addition, I have a strong interest in multi-scale modeling, QM/MM molecular dynamics (QM/MM-MD) simulations of transition-metal systems in complex environments.
